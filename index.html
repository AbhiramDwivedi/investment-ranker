How to read this page

Each name carries three separate statuses, and none of them is a probability.

Evidence status. Verified means the displayed facts and price passed lineage, freshness and independent price checks. Incomplete, conflict and stale mean they did not, and the name waits in the lists of names that are still being verified or stopped until a data repair.

Investment opinion. A buy candidate clears the company-level economic tests at the completed-session price. Watch below means the case holds but the price is too high, so a watch price is shown. No action means one deciding fact failed.

Strategy status. Experimental means the selection rule has no completed forward record yet. Historically supported and forward supported require predeclared return and uncertainty tests.

The strategy is experimental. A buy candidate from an experimental strategy means the current facts and the economic case were checked. It does not establish that the strategy will outperform in the future.

The three valuation cases use Python arithmetic on reported figures and labelled assumptions. A model may review the evidence, but it never supplies a number shown here.

Method. Each weekday after the close, the opportunity-v2 model ranks established, profitable nonfinancial companies on business improvement, economic quality and market confirmation. The top names are then checked one at a time against their SEC filings, an independent price source and three valuation cases. Only names that clear every check appear as cards, and the page never shows more than five.

Honesty box. Every number on this page is computed from filings, prices and stated assumptions. No probability is assigned to any case, and the hurdle is a policy choice rather than a forecast. This page contains general research only and is not investment advice.
