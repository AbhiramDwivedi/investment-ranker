The memo rejects the apparent cheapness: AA is low only on peak-cycle earnings, while its price-to-sales and price-to-book readings sit near the expensive end of their own histories.

## 1. What changed — why is the multiple where it is?

On June 30, 2026, Alcoa agreed to acquire South32's AliGroup interests for $3.1 billion in cash, 17,008,960 shares, a 5% annual ticking fee on the cash consideration and up to $750 million linked to future commodity prices. The deal added integration and dilution risk. On July 16, Alcoa reported Q2 revenue of $3.966 billion and adjusted EBITDA excluding special items of $901 million. The July 30 10-Q reported Total Segment Adjusted EBITDA of $977 million and reduced 2026 alumina production guidance to 9.5 million to 9.6 million metric tons because of Pinjarra instability. Aluminum benefited from higher prices and shipments, while Alumina lost money. On Sept. 1, the S-4 quantified AliGroup's earnings, pro forma leverage and management's approximately $900 million synergy estimate. The 39% drawdown from the 52-week high therefore reflects transaction and execution risk layered onto commodity cyclicality.

## 2. Is the impairment real?

The impairment is partly cyclical and partly operational. Aluminum produced a record $1.073 billion of Q2 segment adjusted EBITDA because realized aluminum reached $4,752 per metric ton and shipments improved. Alumina lost $96 million at a $307-per-metric-ton alumina price index. Management guided to only about $10 million of sequential benefit from Pinjarra stability and lower energy, partly offset by planned maintenance, implying Alumina remains deeply negative absent other changes.

Reported earnings are not normalized. Q2 GAAP EPS was $1.53 versus adjusted EPS of $2.12 after $155 million of net special items. Reported TTM FCF was $352 million versus $304 million after SBC, or 2.3% of market capitalization. First-half operating cash flow was $429 million after a $571 million working-capital use, including a $440 million receivables build tied to higher aluminum prices. Q2 alone produced $608 million of operating cash flow and $422 million of FCF, showing timing helped drive the weak first-half conversion. The hard quality failure is broader: shares rose 49.4% in three years, no Q2 repurchases occurred and the deal adds another 17 million shares.

The S-4 pro forma balance sheet gives $1.160 billion of cash and $5.882 billion of debt, or about $4.722 billion of net debt. Against $3.221 billion of combined normalized EBITDA, leverage is about 1.5x; it becomes dangerous mainly in a trough, where $1.6 billion of EBITDA would put it near 2.9x. The retained $1.360 billion Ma'aden stake remains a partial funding source. AliGroup includes bauxite and alumina assets plus Hillside and Alumar smelter exposure, so it is not purely upstream.

The better peer is the transaction itself. AliGroup's S-4 column shows $871 million of FY2025 segment-EBITDA-equivalent earnings, so approximately $4.1 billion of upfront consideration is about 4.7x, below Alcoa's roughly 6.2x on the memo's normalized standalone base. The filing also shows H1 2026 pro forma diluted EPS of $3.55 versus $3.13 standalone and cites approximately $900 million of synergy NPV, although FY2025 pro forma EPS was lower and the statements exclude synergies. The own-history band still does not establish cheapness because sales and book sit near the expensive ends of their histories.

## 3. Verdict and entry

**Verdict: wait.** The tier is `cyclical_or_single_product`, overriding the engine's `quality_compounder` label, so the hurdle is 12%. Use Q3 2026 earnings as the event gate for Pinjarra output, Alumina losses and the aluminum price deck. The $20.46 machine rung is unreliable because its bases disagree. Pro forma leverage is comfortable, but the price requires a result above base and the implied growth is not achievable without sustained commodity strength.
