1. What changed

Dec. 10, 2025 (Q4/FY25). Revenue $23.77B (+11%), non-GAAP EPS $20.94. Ending ARR $25.20B (+11.5%), revalued $25.66B for FX. FY26 targets: revenue $25.90-26.10B, non-GAAP EPS $23.30-23.50, ARR growth "over 10%" — ex-Semrush.

March 12, 2026 (Q1 FY26). Revenue $6.40B (+12%), non-GAAP EPS $6.06 (+19%), ARR $26.06B (+10.9%); FY26 targets reaffirmed. Same release: Narayen steps down once a successor is appointed, ending 18 years. Shares fell ~7%.

April 2026. Semrush closed Apr. 28 for $1.87B cash; the board added $25B of buyback authority, $26.78B remaining against a ~$104B cap.

June 11, 2026 (Q2 FY26). Revenue $6.618B (+13%, +11% cc), non-GAAP EPS $5.96 (+18%). FY26 revenue and EPS targets raised, to $26.50-26.60B and $24.35-24.45. But FY26 ending ARR growth was set at 10.2% including ~$480M of acquired Semrush ARR — an organic reduction management sized at ~$500M, from deferred Creative Cloud price increases and freemium spend. Same release: CFO Dan Durn departs June 15; $70M goodwill impairment, Publishing & Advertising.

June 12 and July 21, 2026. Downgrades on the CFO exit; Morgan Stanley to Underweight, $365 → $240.

Category (b) growth expectations fell, plus (c) discrete risk. Not (a) — earnings power rose. Not (d) — XLK is 4% off its high.

2. Is the impairment real?

Partly. The price umbrella is conceded; unit demand is not impaired on any disclosed number. But the leading metric was guided down and the two people who would defend it are gone.

The concession is real, and the framing hides it. The FY26 ARR target went from "over 10%" ex-Semrush (Dec. 10) to 10.2% including ~$480M of acquired ARR (June 11): holding the headline while absorbing ~1.9 points of purchased growth is an organic cut of ~two points. Management sized it at ~$500M: deferred Creative Cloud "line optimizations" — planned price increases — plus freemium spend. Deferring a price increase while Canva compounds at ~$4B ARR/+35% and Figma guides to ~30% is a concession however labelled. Fact-check the bull side: "AI-first ARR triples, exceeds $500M" is under 2% of a $27.10B book after three Firefly years.

What is not impaired. Creative & Marketing Professionals subscription revenue — the segment supposedly being eaten — grew 13% (11% cc) to $4.54B in Q2, above Q4 FY25's 10% cc. Gross margin 89.4% against 87.8% three years ago; commoditization would show there first. ROIC 55.4% against 37.6%. Margin guide unchanged at ~45%, fwd_eps_rev_90d +4.22%. Nothing disclosed shows unit loss.

What the price implies (eps_ttm_flag). Trailing 15.1x is unusable; the forward row errs the other way: fwd_at_hurdle_20x_exit +1.29% starts from $27.49 non-GAAP against an FY26 GAAP guide of $17.90-18.00. Use the GAAP row. at_hurdle_20x_exit +5.99%: at a 20x exit, GAAP EPS must compound 5.99% for a decade to clear the 9% hurdle — against Adobe's own 8.65% trailing five-year GAAP CAGR, delivered while the multiple collapsed; $26.78B of buyback authority against a ~$104B cap does much of that. I cannot name a structural reason a business growing 12-13% at 45% margins delivers under 6% GAAP EPS growth for a decade. The band is rejected: every rung sits above spot (upside_to_low_rung +128.5%), pricing a dead growth era.

The replay is the strongest adverse fact. Zero of three beat SPY, median 12-month excess -32.5%. What differs: all three fired at the 23rd-29th percentile, when the absolute forward multiple was 30-40x — cheap only against a bubble. That is the failure mode of a percentile rule in a real reset. Today: 9.6x forward non-GAAP, ~14.7x GAAP, ~10% FCF yield; further compression means pricing terminal decline, not slower growth. That changes the setup, not the safety of the earnings — hence kill criteria on ARR and margin, never multiple.

3. Verdict and entry

Wait — gated on the Sept. 10, 2026 Q3 FY26 report.

Deciding sentence: organic ARR growth ex-Semrush — the one metric that answers the question — was guided down last print, and Q3 absorbs the freemium shift.

No fair_band rung is usable: the low rung sits 128.5% above spot. The gate is an event, not a price. Buying 27 days ahead of that print, +36.5% off the low, pays for information that arrives free. And there is no CEO: a successor can reset guidance or kitchen-sink a quarter.

Tier: quality_compounder, 9%. Gate clean, ROIC 55.4%, net debt/EBITDA 0.15. at_hurdle_20x_exit +5.99% GAAP is achievable if ARR holds near double digits, not if it settles mid-single-digit and the ~45% margin gives back.

4. Kill criteria — pre-committed, falsifiable, dated

  1. Sept. 10, 2026 (Q3 FY26). FY26 ending-ARR growth target cut below 10.2%, or the FY27 ARR framing implies organic (ex-Semrush) growth below 8% → the freemium pivot is not a temporary trade of price for volume. Downgrade to pass.
  2. Sept. 10, 2026. Creative & Marketing Professionals subscription revenue growth below 9% constant currency (Q2 FY26: 11% cc; Q4 FY25: 10% cc) → deceleration has reached the segment the bull case says is intact. Downgrade to pass.
  3. Sept. 10, 2026 or Q4 FY26 (~Dec. 2026). Non-GAAP operating margin guided below 42% for FY27 (FY26 assumption ~45%) → the price concession is being funded out of margin rather than volume, which is the impairment expressed in P&L. Downgrade to pass.
  4. Q1 FY27 (~March 2027). A named permanent CEO who resets FY27 non-GAAP EPS guidance below $24.45 (the FY26 target), or no permanent CEO named by that date → the leadership discount is structural, not transitional. Downgrade to wait indefinitely; re-underwrite.
  5. Any Monday re-check. Quality gate leaves pass, gross margin falls below 87%, or fwd_eps_rev_90d is below -5% for two consecutive runs → the estimate trend has flipped; downgrade pending re-underwrite.
