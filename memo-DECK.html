What this memo argues with. One thing, and it is decisive: the band. Its low rung, $117.43, sits 30.3% ABOVE the $90.11 close. Under the template's own rule that makes it not an entry rung at all.

The replay is not argued with: three episodes, 67% beating SPY by a median 67%, but the most recent one (March 2025) lost to SPY by 37%, which is the honest warning that the last time this name looked this cheap the cheapness kept going.

1. What changed — why is the multiple where it is?

Deckers is at the 1st percentile of its own decade on P/E and the 1.4th blended, and nothing in the filings looks like a break. It looks like a growth-rate reset, in four dated steps.

  • FY2026 (year ended Mar. 31, 2026; 10-K filed May 22, 2026). Net sales +9.8% to $5,472.3M, +9.0% constant currency — the first single-digit year after a run of 15%-plus. HOKA +15.9% to $2,587.3M, UGG +8.2% to $2,738.8M, Other brands −33.9% to $146.2M on the Koolaburra phase-out. Diluted EPS $7.02, +10.9%. Operating margin 23.1%, down 50bp.
  • The U.S. stopped growing. Domestic net sales rose 0.2% in FY2026 — the whole year's growth was international (+26.8%). That is the single most important line in the file, and it predates the de-rating.
  • Q1 FY2027 (quarter ended Jun. 30, 2026; released Jul. 23, 10-Q filed Jul. 30). Net sales +5.7% to $1,019.5M (+4.8% cc). HOKA +7.7% to $703.5M. UGG +4.9% to $278.0M. Domestic +3.2%, international +8.4%. Gross margin 56.4%, up 60bp. But SG&A rose 12.7% to $419.9M, so operating income fell 6.0% to $155.3M and operating margin fell 190bp to 15.2%. Diluted EPS $0.94, +1.1%.
  • Tariffs, quantified. The 10-Q says the company has paid about $120.0M gross in IEEPA tariffs, has begun filing for refunds, has recognized none of it, and does not expect mitigation to fully offset the incremental tariff cost this fiscal year. FY2027 guidance excludes any refund.

So: (b) growth expectations fell and (a) earnings power is being squeezed at the margin line, together. Not (c) — there is no discrete risk event in the filings — and not only (d), though a −5.9% XLY against DECK's −28.2% says some of it is the sector too.

2. Is the impairment real?

Partly, and the market is pricing much more than "partly." Take the three pieces separately.

The U.S. slowdown is real and is not a one-quarter artifact. Domestic sales were +0.2% for FY2026 and +3.2% in Q1 FY2027. HOKA's decelerating from +15.9% to +7.7% while management guides the full year to a low-double-digit increase (guidance of Jul. 23, 2026, reaffirmed at that level) means the guide requires a re-acceleration in the back half that has not started. That is a falsifiable claim with a date on it, and it is the most likely thing to break.

The margin compression is real but is being disclosed as tariff-driven and partially reversible. FY2027 guidance: revenue $5.86-5.91B, gross margin slightly better than 56.5%, SG&A about 35% of sales, operating margin slightly better than 21.5%, EPS $7.35-$7.50. Use the midpoint: $7.425, up 5.8% on FY2026's $7.02, with about 160bp of operating margin given away. The $120.0M of IEEPA tariffs already paid is a real cash cost carried at zero recognized recovery — that is conservative accounting, not an impairment of the franchise, and any refund is upside nobody is booking.

Nothing in the quality or capital picture is impaired. Gross margin is 57.8% against 50.9% three years ago. ROIC is 84.5%, up from 69.8%. Net cash, no outstanding borrowings, $1.603B of cash, inventories DOWN year over year to $807.6M — inventory is not backing up behind the slowdown, which is what a real demand break looks like. The company bought back 3.3 million shares for $338.2M in the June quarter alone (vs $183.0M a year earlier) at an average $103.79, and guides to spending about 80% of FY2027 free cash flow on buybacks against a $4.7B remaining authorization.

What the price implies. At the 9% quality-compounder hurdle and an exit at its own median 23.2x, the $90.11 close implies +2.65% EPS growth a year for ten years. Against trailing five-year EPS CAGR of +22.4%, consensus +11.5% for next year, and management's own guided +5.8% for this year — with a 10% buyback yield that alone funds about 10% of share-count-driven EPS growth a year. The price is not asking whether growth slows; it is asking whether growth essentially stops, permanently, at a company that has already told you it will retire a tenth of its shares annually. I cannot name the structural reason for that, and the filings do not supply one. The nameable risks — a U.S. HOKA plateau, tariffs, wholesale over-shipping — are all worth several points of growth, not all of it.

The honest counterweight is the replay: the last time DECK entered this zone (March 2025) it went on to lag SPY by 37% over the following year. Cheap on its own history has been a poor short-run signal for this name once already.

3. Verdict and entry

Wait — but the reason is different from the one on file, and it is worth recording plainly.

The band cannot be used as the entry rung here. fair_band.low is $117.43 against a $90.11 close (upside_to_low_rung +30.3%), so the p20 rung sits above today's price; the template's own rule says do not arm a "margin of safety" above spot. That leaves spot or an event gate.

The gate that would actually matter is an event, not a price: the H2 FY2027 re-acceleration in HOKA, first testable at the Q2 print on Oct. 22, 2026 and decided at Q3 in late January 2027. Tier is 9% (quality compounder — diversified across two brands, three channels, two geographies, net cash, 57.8% gross margin); at that tier the price implies +2.65% and the company guides +5.8% this year, so the hurdle is achievable on the arithmetic without heroic assumptions.

4. Kill criteria — pre-committed, falsifiable, dated

  1. HOKA constant-currency growth below +5% in two straight quarters, or any negative quarter. Checked on each quarterly release; next Oct. 22, 2026. Q1 FY2027 reported +7.7% (as-reported; the release gives cc only at the consolidated level, +4.8%) — PASS, with under 3 points of room.
  2. H2 FY2027 re-acceleration missed — full-year HOKA lands below a low-double-digit increase, or the FY2027 revenue guide of $5.86-5.91B is cut. Checked Oct. 22, 2026 and late January 2027.
  3. Full-year operating margin below 19.5%. Guided "slightly better than 21.5%" on Jul. 23, 2026. Checked at each guidance update.
  4. Wholesale outrunning DTC by more than 5 points over a fiscal year. FY2026 TRIPPED this: wholesale +12.3% vs DTC +6.3%, a 6.0-point gap. Q1 FY2027 reversed it hard — DTC +13.0% vs wholesale +2.2%, comparable DTC +6.8%. Re-checked at FY2027 year-end, May 2027; a second consecutive fiscal year of it is a downgrade, not a discussion.
  5. Buybacks paused or debt-funded. Currently the opposite: $338.2M in the June quarter, no outstanding borrowings, $4.7B authorization remaining. Checked each 10-Q.
