This memo argues with the fair band at all three rungs, not only the low one, and with the replay. It does not argue with the quality gate.

1. What changed

The multiple sits at the 9th percentile of its own decade while the stock trades 2.4% below its 52-week high. Both are true because earnings moved, not price.

(a) Earnings power rose, hard. Q2 2026 (July 30): revenue $1.308B, +13% reported / +12% organic; non-GAAP gross margin 64.1%, up 590bps; non-GAAP operating margin 25.1%; non-GAAP EPS $0.70 against $0.48 a year earlier and $0.61 consensus. TTM EPS is +75% year over year. The stock rose about 16.6% on the print, about 55% over three months.

(b) Growth was re-based years ago and has not recovered. U.S. revenue grew 11% in Q1 2026 and 11% in Q2 2026; organic total growth was 12% in both. Trailing five-year revenue CAGR is 18.0%.

(c) A discrete, dated risk appeared. CMS's Nov. 28, 2025 DMEPOS final rule puts CGMs into the next competitive-bidding round and moves them to monthly rental. Bidding opens around now; single payment amounts take effect no later than Jan. 1, 2028.

(d) Sector rotation: none. XLV is 0.6% off its own 52-week high.

2. Is the impairment real?

The de-rating was deserved, and it is already finished. DexCom's own P/E history — p20 47x, median 100x; P/FCF p20 53x, median 127x — was paid for a company compounding revenue in the mid-20s. This one guides 11-13% for 2026 and told investors at its May 14, 2026 Investor Day it will do "10%+ organic every year through 2030," with 2030 non-GAAP gross margin 67-69% and operating margin 29-30%. A 12% organic grower does not get 100x back. The mid rung ($253) is an artifact, and the low rung ($119, 38x forward EPS) sits 32% above spot and above the consensus target of $94.12. The whole band is unusable — arming at fair_band.low would be arming a buy at today's price and calling it a margin of safety.

The replay warning describes exactly this mistake. Four entries into the ≤30th-percentile zone since 2022, 25% beat SPY, median excess −30%, median 12-month drawdown −35%. Three of the four (Jan. 2023, Aug. 2023, Feb. 2024) bought a percentile computed against a growth-era denominator while the growth rate decayed underneath it. What is genuinely different now is direction: gross margin recovered to 64.1% non-GAAP, operating margin to 25.1%, 90-day forward EPS revisions +2.8% with 14 net raises, new patient starts a global record in Q1 with Q2 in line. What is not different, and is why this is not a buy: those four failures were entries at a low price into a deteriorating franchise. This would be an entry at the 52-week high into a recovered one, on the same broken yardstick.

The only usable anchor is the 20x-exit reverse DCF. At $89.75, earning the 9% hurdle over ten years with a 20x terminal multiple requires EPS to compound 13.0%/yr from the $3.12 forward base (15.5% from the $2.52 TTM base). Against that: trailing five-year EPS CAGR 13.3%, consensus +17.4% next year, and management's plan. The plan clears the bar through 2030 — 10%+ revenue with operating margin going 23.5-24% to 29-30% is mid-teens EPS growth. It does not clear it for a decade, because the margin lever is spent by 2030 and Medicare reprices from Jan. 1, 2028; the FY2025 10-K concedes reimbursement "will decrease beginning in 2028." The price requires management's plan to work and then keep working for five years past its own horizon, through a repricing the company has already conceded. That is not an impaired business. It is a good business at a price that leaves nothing for error.

Two bull claims survive the check. DexCom outgrew Abbott in CGM in Q2 2026: +13% reported against Abbott's +11.0% reported / +9.5% comparable (Abbott release, July 16, 2026). GLP-1 is a tailwind on the company's own CONNECT data — CEO Jacob Leach: "The largest relative improvement in A1C was within the cohort using only GLP-1 therapies." Against that: the March 4, 2025 FDA warning letter (San Diego and Mesa; a resistance-layer component change that made sensors less accurate) is 17 months open with no close-out found; Stelo is still immaterial ("you can count the millions for the year on one hand" — CFO Jereme Sylvain); and Sylvain guided gross margin to "peak into Q3" then step down as Ireland comes online.

3. Verdict and entry

WAIT. The sentence that decides it: the cheapness is real only against the wrong yardstick — a 12% grower measured on a 25% grower's multiple history — and against the one absolute anchor, $89.75 already demands 13%/yr EPS growth for ten years at a 20x exit, more than management's own 2030 plan delivers past 2030.

Entry: event gate, no price rung. Not fair_band.low ($119), which is above spot and above consensus target. Not spot. The gate is the Q3 2026 print (Oct. 29, 2026) together with the CMS non-insulin type-2 coverage decision management expects "before the end of this year," effective "in the middle of 2027." Coverage without a U.S. growth break above the three-quarter 11% plateau is a press release, not a re-rating.

Tier. The engine applied quality_compounder (9%). Sensors are essentially the whole company and one payor regime resets the reference price each bidding cycle, which reads as cyclical_or_single_product (12%) under the return-hurdle policy's own rule. The verdict survives either way: even the softer 9% tier needs 13.0%/yr for a decade, which §2 cannot underwrite.

4. Kill criteria — dated and falsifiable

  1. U.S. revenue growth below +9% YoY in any quarter through Q4 2027 — "Revenue by Geography" table, quarterly earnings 8-K Ex. 99.1. First check Oct. 29, 2026. Three straight quarters at 11%; the case needs ~10%+.
  2. CMS declines or narrows non-insulin type-2 CGM coverage, or the decision slips past Dec. 31, 2026 — CMS Medicare coverage database, plus the Q4 2026 call (~Feb. 2027). Management staked the 2030 plan on this.
  3. Competitive-bidding single payment amount implying a >20% Medicare CGM price cut — CMS SPA announcement, contracting 2026-27, effective Jan. 1, 2028.
  4. Non-GAAP gross margin below 62.0% for two consecutive quarters — earnings 8-K. The entire 29-30% 2030 operating-margin target is a gross-margin bridge, and the CFO has already flagged a Q3 peak and an Ireland step-down.
  5. A second FDA warning letter, or any Class I recall of a Dexcom sensor, on or before Dec. 31, 2027 — FDA warning-letter and device-recall databases.
