1. What changed

May 20, 2026 (Q3 FY26, 10-Q and call). Revenue $8.6 billion, up 10%. Intuit raised full-year guidance to $21.341-$21.374 billion (13-14% growth) and non-GAAP EPS to $23.80-$23.85 (18% growth). The same release cut the full-time workforce roughly 17%, booking $300 million-$340 million of restructuring charges primarily in Q4. CEO Sasan Goodarzi on DIY tax: "We faced pressure among the most price-sensitive DIY filers earning less than $50,000 annually. We lost on price." TurboTax Online paying units are guided to grow 2%. Total IRS filers are expected to fall about 30 basis points, roughly 2 million units below macro expectations — "the most significant industry-wide contraction since the post-COVID tax season."

May 21. Shares fell 20.02%, $383.93 to $307.07.

June 2. Goldman Sachs cut to Sell, target $519 to $276, arguing AI models process a return for $0.12 against TurboTax's roughly $162 of revenue per return, and modeling TurboTax revenue 18% below fiscal 2025 by 2030 if 20% of filers move to fully AI preparation.

June 22. 52-week low, $252.84. Aug. 12. Securities class action filed, Baldwin v. Intuit, N.D. Cal. 26-cv-7086, lead-plaintiff deadline Sept. 8.

Category (b), growth expectations fell, plus (c), a discrete competitive risk appeared. Not (a) — earnings power rose. Not (d) — XLK sits 4% off its high while INTU sits 52% off its own.

2. Is the impairment real?

Partly. The real part is small; the market prices it as total.

The concession is real. Management does not dispute it — it will "evolve our DIY business model to deliver the right value at the right price point" at the low end. That is a margin give-back, not a costless defense. The named entrants launched into the 2026 season and are credible: Prime Meridian, free federal and state filing, with former IRS commissioner Danny Werfel advising; Perplexity's tax agent at $17 a month. Goldman's $0.12-versus-$162 asymmetry is a real cost-structure problem in a commoditizable product.

The scope is not. Intuit sizes DIY at a $5 billion TAM — 12% of the TurboTax TAM. The assisted category is $37 billion, or 88%, and that is where the business went: TurboTax Live revenue up 36% and customers up 38% in fiscal 2026, now 53% of total TurboTax revenue, up 11 points year over year. That is growth in the segment least exposed to a chatbot, because a human expert signs the return and carries the liability. TurboTax is itself only about $5.28 billion of a $21.35 billion company; Global Business Solutions grew 15% in Q3, Online Ecosystem 19%, mid-market above 30%. A problem inside a slice of one-quarter of revenue is being discounted as a whole-company terminal break.

Two bear claims fail on the filings. The regulatory overhang is stale — on March 20, 2026, the Fifth Circuit vacated the FTC's order and remanded. The balance sheet is not stressed: net debt/EBITDA 0.02x, nine-month operating cash flow $7.507 billion on $148 million of capex, refinanced in June at 4.95% and 5.50%.

What the price implies (eps_ttm_flag). Trailing P/E is unusable here — TTM GAAP EPS of $16.50 sits far below the $27.32 forward base, and the Q4 restructuring charge will push it lower still. Use the absolute anchor: at $345.66, exiting at a plain 20x, the buyer earns the 9% hurdle if forward EPS compounds 2.29% a year. Consensus next-year growth is 14.63%; trailing five-year EPS CAGR 16.17%; CFO Sandeep Aujla committed to "annual EPS growth of at least mid-teens over the coming years" at the same event that cost the stock a fifth of its value. I cannot name a structural reason DIY repricing takes a company growing revenue 13-14% down to 2%/yr EPS for a decade. That gap is the case. Discount it honestly: $27.32 is non-GAAP against a GAAP guide of $15.79-$15.84, mostly stock compensation, so true required growth exceeds 2.29% — wide enough to absorb, not wide enough to buy blind into a print.

The band (caveats). Rejected as an entry rung: p20 is a 40.91x growth-era P/E, and low_implied_fwd_pe 24.7 asks 24.7x forward earnings for a business whose terminal multiple is the entire dispute. The band also sits roughly 2x spot (discount_to_low -48.8%). A band saying "buy at $675" while the stock trades at $346 imposes no discipline; it is broken here, not bullish.

The replay. Not counted as support. The 75% hit rate is a median of four. The one closed episode inside this de-rating — April 10, 2025, at the 29.8th percentile — lost 36% and trailed SPY by 69.9 points. Both open episodes (Nov. 2025, Jan. 2026) are underwater and excluded from the hit rate. What is different now: the tax-season result is disclosed and the percentile is 2.2, not 29.8. The bad news that broke the April 2025 entry has happened. Reason to look again, not evidence the trade works.

3. Verdict and entry

Memo-buy candidate, gated on the Aug. 25 print.

The deciding sentence: the price implies low-single-digit EPS growth for a decade, management guides at least mid-teens, and the only impairment I can document sits in a segment worth 12% of the TurboTax TAM inside a business worth a quarter of revenue.

Entry: spot ($345.66) or better, executable only after the Aug. 25 fiscal Q4 report. No fair_band rung is used — every rung sits above spot and embeds a growth-era multiple (§2). The gate is a date, not a price, because Aug. 25 delivers what does not yet exist: fiscal 2027 guidance, the first time management prices the DIY repricing and the restructuring savings together. Buying now pays for that print after a 36% bounce off the low, at RSI 64, with 67% volatility and no edge.

Tier: quality_compounder, 9% — diversified across QuickBooks, TurboTax and Credit Karma; ROE 22.5%; FCF margin 36.9%; gate passes with no hard or soft fails. Terminal uncertainty is elevated, but the return-hurdle policy forbids raising the hurdle to express qualitative doubt; it goes in §4 instead.

4. Kill criteria

  1. Aug. 25, 2026 (Q4/FY26 report). Fiscal 2027 non-GAAP EPS guidance midpoint implies growth below 10% vs the $23.80-$23.85 FY26 guide → management's "at least mid-teens" commitment is abandoned. Downgrade to pass.
  2. Aug. 25, 2026. Fiscal 2027 revenue growth guidance midpoint below 8% (FY26: 13-14%) → the damage is not confined to DIY. Downgrade to pass.
  3. Q1 FY27 report (~late Nov. 2026). Online Ecosystem revenue growth below 12% year over year (Q3 FY26: 19%) → AI pressure has migrated from consumer tax into the business platform. That is the structural case, and it kills the thesis.
  4. Q3 FY27 report (~May 2027). TurboTax Live revenue growth below 15% — the floor of management's own stated 15-20% long-term range, against 36% in FY26 → the assisted moat, which is the whole bull case, is eroding.
  5. Any Monday re-check. Quality gate leaves pass, or fwd_eps_rev_90d below -5% for two consecutive runs → downgrade to wait pending a re-underwrite.
