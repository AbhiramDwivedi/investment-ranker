## 3. Verdict and entry (<=150 words)

**Wait.** The price is not paying for the single-customer/second-source risk,
and last week added a governance risk the machine's own-history math has
never priced; neither is resolved by the Q3 print alone, even though
ordinary earnings power (section 2) is closer to today's peer multiples than
the raw GAAP comparison suggested.

The quality-compounder tier (9% hurdle) is right: three segments, no single
program dominant, though 74% of YTD 2026 revenue is U.S. Government-linked
(10-Q MD&A) - that belongs in the growth path, not a hurdle bump.
fair_band.low ($279.30) sits 4.7% above spot (upside_to_low_rung positive),
so it is not a margin-of-safety rung; entry stays an event gate.

**The AXYV preferred, sized.** Two mutually exclusive branches (10-Q Note
B), neither zero: (1) No-QIPO/redemption - the $968M carrying value already
accretes toward the disclosed $1,422M "highest redemption value"; net debt
of $9,478M (7/3/26) becomes a $10,900M debt-equivalent claim once the
preferred is added at that value, a claim worth roughly $7.62/share (over
186.5M common shares) ahead of common equity, before the 7% cumulative
quarterly compounding that grows it further each quarter outstanding and
before the 110% redemption-premium methodology that applies if contractual
milestones are not met by 2027-12-31. (2) QIPO/conversion - the DoW investor
converts to ~10% of AXYV plus up to 3% fully diluted via warrants; at MSL's
FY26E guide (~$4.1B revenue x low-12% margin, ~$492M operating income), a
10-13% minority claim is ~$49-64M pretax, ~$0.22-0.29/share after tax at the
14.4% ETR - a real but modest drag on the normalized EPS above, well short
of a full loss of the choke point. Neither branch sits inside the scenario
table below, which prices 100%-owned Missile Solutions; both are real claims
against the common shares the table does not carry.

Absent that disclosure, this stays at wait past Q3 even if the guide holds.
