2. Is the impairment real?

Both. The de-rating is deserved as a re-rating, the franchise is not impaired, and the 7th percentile is partly manufactured.

The manufactured part. TTM EPS $3.18 = $0.59 + $0.56 + $1.23 + $0.80; TTM FCF $11.152B holds Q1'26's $5,094M. Q1'26 carries the WBD money: the 10-Q books "a $2.8 billion termination fee received during the first quarter of 2026"; the cash ran through operations. So two of three cheapness legs — P/E 7.2, P/FCF 4.3 — sit on an inflated base, and the fee is why consensus_eps_growth_1y reads +6.39%. Strip it and 2027 consensus growth is mid-twenties, not six. eps_ttm_flag catches TTM far below forward; this is the opposite error, unflagged.

What is structural. Volume growth is finished: view hours +2% in H1'26, +1.5% in 2025, per Netflix's own report. UCAN grew 10% in Q2'26 with an in-quarter price increase. Nielsen has YouTube at a record 13.8% of US TV time in May 2026, from 10.8% a year earlier; Netflix 8.0%. View hours go annual in 2027, the second engagement metric retired in two years. Growth is now price plus ads, both with ceilings volume never had; a 50x multiple does not survive that.

What is not. Operating income guided +20%+ for 2026 at 31.5% margin vs 29.5%; Q3'26 33.2% vs 28.2%. ROIC 30.1% vs 12.4% three years ago. Netflix refused to pay $82.7B for Warner Bros. — the stock rose 13.8% next session — then repurchased $4.7B in Q2'26 at a dollar-weighted 37th percentile of own history. Ads roughly double to ~$3B, ~6% of revenue. Content cost is the weakest bear leg: FY2025 cash spend rose 5.4% to $17.1B, and 2026's step to ~1.1x amortization buys live sport — 5% of spend, six of five years' ten biggest sign-up days. Not a broken franchise.

The price. At $78.16, a 20x exit at the 9% hurdle needs +9.27% forward EPS CAGR for a decade — more, since the TTM base is inflated. Revenue ~10% decaying, margin to the high-30s and a 2-3% buyback delivers that. Priced at a mature multiple, not below one.

Band and replay, rejected. caveats is empty, but p20 is 38.0x earnings and 39.4x free cash flow — growth-era rungs off the fee-inflated P/FCF leg — and upside_to_low_rung is +32%. Replay: 40% hit rate, median 12m excess -11.3%, 0-for-2 at 36 months; the deepest prior entry (Mar. 2020) gained 38% and still lost SPY by 14.9 points. What is different now — a growth-rate de-rating, not macro — cuts against.

3. Verdict and entry

Wait.

The sentence: at $78.16 the price already asks roughly the growth an honest base case delivers, and the two legs that make the screen scream cheap rest on a $2.8B receipt that will not repeat.

Entry: no band rung. upside_to_low_rung is +32% — $103.16 sits above spot, so it is not a margin-of-safety rung, and it comes off the fee-inflated P/FCF leg besides. Event gate instead: the January 2027 Q4'26 report, when Netflix first guides FY2027 revenue growth and operating margin. That pair separates deceleration-to-a-floor from deceleration-continuing; §2 turns on it. The Q3'26 print (expected ~Oct. 20, unconfirmed) is an interim check, not an entry.

Tier: quality_compounder, 9% — gate passes clean, ROIC 30.1%, net debt 0.51x EBITDA. fwd_at_hurdle_20x_exit +9.27% is achievable on §2, without cushion.

4. Kill criteria — pre-committed, falsifiable, dated

  1. Q3'26 report (~Oct. 20, 2026). Q4'26 revenue growth guidance below 11% F/X-neutral, against the 11% F/X-neutral Q3 guide → the deceleration has no floor. Downgrade to pass.
  2. Q3'26 report (~Oct. 20, 2026). FY2026 operating margin guidance cut below 31.5%, or FY2026 FCF guidance cut below ~$12.5B → the margin-and-cash engine that must replace volume growth is the entire remaining bull case, and it is breaking. Downgrade to pass.
  3. Q4'26 report + FY2027 guidance (~Jan. 2027). FY2027 revenue growth guidance midpoint below 10%, OR FY2027 operating margin guidance below 33.5% (under 200bp of expansion off 31.5%) → price plus ads cannot replace volume. This kills the thesis outright.
  4. What We Watched, annual edition (Q1 2027). Full-year 2026 view-hours growth below +1.5% (2025: +1.5%; H1'26: +2%) → engagement is receding and the pricing runway closes with it. This is now the only volume disclosure left, so it is checked once a year and it is binding.
  5. Any Monday re-check. FY2027 ads revenue guidance, when first given, implying growth below 40% (2026: ~+100% to ~$3B); OR quality gate leaves pass; OR fwd_eps_rev_90d below -3% for two consecutive runs → downgrade pending re-underwrite.
