2. Is the impairment real?

The mix argument holds. The growth argument does not. The de-rating is about half earned.

Units. New in-ground construction, management's estimates: 117,000 units in 2021, 98,000 in 2022, 62,000 in 2024, just under 60,000 in 2025, ~58,000 guided for 2026 — down half. Revenue was not: down 14% from the 2022 peak of $6.18B, then within 1% of $5.3B for three years. Mix says why — new construction was 15–20% of industry spend in 2021 and is 14% of sales in 2025, while maintenance and minor repair went from "almost 60%" to 64%. The 2021 10-K notes maintenance rose to ~70% in the 2006–2010 bust; the installed base absorbed this one too.

The base reset off a bubble, not below trend. Versus 2019: revenue $5.36B TTM vs $3.20B, operating income $585M vs $341M, operating margin 10.9% vs 10.7%. The 2022 peak — 16.6% margins, $18.60 EPS — was the anomaly.

What is impaired is the growth rate. Trailing 5-year EPS CAGR +0.45%. Q2 2026: chemicals -2% on price, building materials +4%, total +2%. Watwood, July 23, on the historical 6–9% algorithm: "under current market conditions, I don't think the six to nine is on the table." ROIC 16.6% versus 29.0% three years ago. The empty caveats array is wrong: the window holds a 52.35x maximum from 2020–21, and no-growth distributors do not earn 29.79x. The mid rung ($324.71, 27.5x forward) is not a target; the p20 rung ($251.57, 21.3x forward) is — above spot.

What the price asks. eps_ttm_flag is null and deservedly so — TTM EPS $10.90 is GAAP, the forward $11.82 near-GAAP — so the reverse-DCF needs no haircut. At a 20x exit and the 9% hurdle the price requires +5.45%/yr. Buyback capacity scores 5.01%, but H1 2026 repurchases were $86M against $341M in 2025, FCF conversion 0.77 against a 1.46 three-year average, debt +$110.8M. Reachable, not free.

The replay argues against. The May 2022 entry at the 0.2nd percentile trailed SPY by 11.9 points over 12 months, 62.3 over 36; the open Sept. 2025 entry is -53.5%. Both failed the same way: the denominator was still falling ($18+ trailing EPS in May 2022, then -42%). What is different — FY25 landed at $10.85 and FY26 is guided $10.87–$11.17, confirmed twice after two cuts in 2025. The E has stopped falling. A second look, not a position: estimates still drift down (-1.8% in 30 days, 7 net downgrades).

3. Verdict and entry

Wait.

The deciding sentence: the franchise is intact and the cheapness is only half explained, but the one input that sets the multiple — what growth this business can now do — is exactly what management withdrew when it postponed the Investor Day with no new date, four months into a CEO's tenure, having just said the old algorithm is off the table.

Entry: no fair_band rung. upside_to_low_rung is +29.1% — the p20 rung sits 29% above spot: an upside marker, not a margin of safety. Gate on the rescheduled Investor Day, or failing that the Oct. 22, 2026 Q3 print; re-underwrite at spot or better.

Tier: quality_compounder, 9% — gate passes clean, ROE 31.3%, 64% of sales non-discretionary. Generous given §2, but +5.45% at a 20x exit is achievable; hence wait, not pass.
