## 1. What changed — why is the multiple where it is? (≤ 200 words)

On Aug. 5, 2026, Permian Resources reported second-quarter oil production of 198,071 barrels per day, up 3% sequentially, and raised full-year oil guidance to a 199,000-barrel-per-day midpoint while widening the cash capital-spending range to $1.9 billion to $2.0 billion. Total company production fell to 376,409 barrels of oil equivalent per day from 385,118 a year earlier, entirely a natural-gas story: NGL volumes fell 12% and gas volumes fell 17% as the company curtailed high-gas wells during a Waha price collapse that made second-quarter natural gas sales negative $120.7 million (negative $139.2 million for the six months). The 10-Q filed Aug. 6 showed why the quarter's earnings looked so strong: oil realized $97.81 per barrel, and a $256.6 million noncash derivative gain helped lift GAAP diluted EPS to $0.93 versus adjusted EPS of $0.69. Year to date the company also closed about 190 ground-game transactions for roughly $530 million and, separately, a $520.0 million Ward County bolt-on that closed July 31, after quarter-end -- together the $1.05 billion figure often quoted for the half. This is stronger oil pricing and a larger acquisition program, not a new impairment. The shares sit only 2.1% below their 52-week high.

## 2. Is the impairment real? (the memo's only real job, ≤ 400 words)

There is no impairment to defend; the question is whether today's price already pays for a cyclical peak. One segment, so no blended-multiple problem to decompose.

**Oil economics.** First-half oil volume rose 11% and realized oil price rose 27%, lifting oil and gas sales 26% -- but total volume fell 2.3% (376,409 Boe/d versus 385,118), so section 0's rev_yoy 55.1% is a price artifact, not production growth. Six-month LOE held flat at $5.36/Boe; the Q2 rise to $5.55/Boe is a curtailment-shrunk denominator, not cost inflation (absolute LOE flat: $190.0mm versus $188.0mm). H1 development capital ($987.7mm) was funded entirely from operating cash flow.

**Gas and Ward County.** Natural gas sales were negative $120.7 million in Q2, negative $139.2 million for the half; curtailment and firm transport mitigate Waha exposure, not eliminate it. The $1.05 billion year-to-date "ground game" blends about $530 million of true small-deal activity (190 deals, no existing production by design) with a separate $520.0 million Ward County bolt-on -- roughly half the dollars in one deal. H1 depletion of $14.37/Boe versus $14.29 a year earlier shows no overpayment signal, but the acreage is too new to judge on returns.

**Cash flow, corrected.** Section 0's fcf_ttm ($3,991,577,000, a stated 20.1% SBC-adjusted yield) is TTM operating cash flow with no capex deducted -- it reconciles exactly to FY2025 operating cash flow less H1 2025 plus H1 2026. The company's own adjusted free cash flow, summed from four releases, is $2,135.6 million TTM (Q3'25 $468.8mm, Q4'25 $403.1mm, Q1'26 $512.7mm, Q2'26 $751.0mm) -- a 10.7% yield against a roughly $20.0 billion market cap, about half the reported figure. Six-month SBC of $35.5 million is immaterial against that base (about 3.3% annualized), so the SBC conclusion survives; the yield used to reach it does not, and sbc_adjusted_fcf/sbc_to_fcf inherit the same error.

**Peer and tier.** Normalized TTM adjusted EPS of $1.82 puts PR at 12.8x, not the reported 15.5x. Matador trades at 10.1x; across the wider shale cohort (SM 7.2x, APA 9.0x, CHRD 9.8x, MTDR 10.1x, EOG 11.3x, DVN 11.4x), PR sits above every direct comparable, partly offset by lower leverage (0.5x net debt-to-LQA EBITDAX versus higher peer ratios). Section 0's `quality_compounder` tier is the engine's default for every energy name checked -- FANG, DVN, OVV, SM, CHRD, APA, EOG, COP and MUR carry it too, not a considered classification. A single-basin, single-commodity pure-play is `cyclical_or_single_product`; section 3 uses its 12% hurdle.

4. Kill criteria — pre-committed, falsifiable, dated (3-5 lines)

  • Initial 2027 oil-production guidance below 200,000 Bbls/d while cash capital expenditure guidance is above $2.0 billion is a first flag; below 195,000 Bbls/d while capex is above $2.1 billion is the re-underwrite trigger. Checked when initial 2027 guidance is issued, expected with Q4 2026 earnings in February 2027. A disclosed weather outage, pipeline force majeure, or voluntary gas curtailment with unchanged per-well productivity is a benign exception.
  • Total controllable cash costs (LOE, GP&T and cash G&A) exceed $7.90 per Boe for two consecutive quarters, checked at each quarterly release through 2027: re-underwrite the claimed cost advantage. Cost per Boe rising because voluntary high-gas-ratio curtailment shrank the Boe denominator while absolute controllable cash cost dollars stayed flat, or a one-quarter storm or pipeline outage, are benign exceptions.
  • Total debt, net exceeds $3.5 billion, or unproved-property impairment expense exceeds $75 million in a single quarter, checked at each quarterly release through 2027: re-underwrite acquisition and capital-allocation discipline. A signed, individually disclosed accretive acquisition with a specific stated deleveraging plan, or a portfolio-wide high-grading decision unrelated to the 2026-2027 ground-game program, is a benign exception.
  • Diluted weighted-average shares outstanding rise more than 2% year over year from the 855.2 million reported for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 (the baseline now that the Class C-to-A conversion is complete) without a disclosed stock-funded acquisition, checked at each 10-Q through 2027: re-underwrite per-share compounding.
  • Realized oil price stays below $65 per barrel for two consecutive quarters while the company's own adjusted (capex-deducted) free cash flow turns negative, checked at each quarterly earnings release through 2027: re-underwrite the cyclical and valuation thesis entirely. Realized price suppressed by hedge-settlement timing while unhedged realizations and the forward strip both stay above $65 is a benign exception.
