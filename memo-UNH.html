  1. DOJ/regulatory MA-billing review - tiered by disclosure, not by a single pass/fail line. No update in a filing leaves the memo's wait/event gate unresolved rather than cleared. Any disclosed loss accrual, deferred- prosecution agreement, exclusion/monitor terms, or a filed disclosure that materially narrows permissible risk-adjustment or chart-review practices (even with no accrual or charge) triggers review, with severity set by size against normalized net earnings (>$16.75B guided) and whether remedies change business practices versus a fine alone. Formal criminal charges against the company or a named executive trigger re-underwrite regardless of amount. Checked at each quarterly filing's Legal Matters note, next the Q3 2026 10-Q (expected early November 2026).
  2. Medical care ratio - same-quarter year-over-year, AND read against segment guidance so a membership-driven improvement is not mistaken for pricing adequacy. Q1 is consistently UNH's seasonal low (83.9% in 2026, 84.8% in 2025); Q3 has run higher every year on file (89.9% in 2025). Q3 2026 MCR at or above Q3 2025's 89.9% triggers re-underwrite; improvement under ~1.3 points (half of Q2's 2.7-point year-over-year gain) triggers review; consolidated MCR improving while UnitedHealthcare segment operating-earnings guidance is flat or cut (membership/mix doing the work, not pricing) triggers review; FY26 MCR guidance (88.1% +/-25bps) raised above 88.35% triggers review. Checked at the Oct. 27, 2026 Q3 print.
  3. Reserve development quality - dollars and share of the reported medical-cost base, on a comparable period basis, with unfavorable development requiring corroboration before it re-underwrites. Track Note 4's favorable/unfavorable prior-year development, in dollars and as a percent of that period's total reported medical costs, six-months-vs- six-months or full-year-vs-full-year (H1 2026: $1,250M, 0.8% of $148.8B; H1 2025: $320M). The figure remaining flat or growing while its percent share of reported medical costs also grows triggers review. Unfavorable development (negative) in any period triggers review by itself, and re-underwrite only if corroborated by any of: widening days claims payable, a current-period (ex-prior-development) MCR miss versus guidance, or a cut to segment operating-earnings guidance. Checked at each 10-Q/10-K, next the Q3 2026 10-Q.
  4. MA and Optum Health membership - year-over-year AND sequential, read with segment earnings so an intentional, profitable exit is not treated the same as broad-based demand loss.
