This memo argues with four things: the null eps_ttm_flag and the empty fair_band.caveats (both are errors — §2), the spread in fair_band_by_basis, and the replay.

1. What changed

(a) Earnings power fell, then recovered. May 13, 2025: UNH suspended 2025 guidance, citing Medicare Advantage costs for new members "higher than expected," and replaced CEO Andrew Witty with Stephen Hemsley; the stock fell about 18% in a day [1]. Full-year 2025 (Jan. 27, 2026): revenue $447.6 billion, up 12%; GAAP EPS $13.23; adjusted EPS $16.35; net margin 2.7%. Initial 2026 guidance: revenue above $439.0 billion — below 2025 — adjusted EPS above $17.75, MCR 88.8% ±50 bps [2]. Two raises followed: April 21, 2026, Q1 adjusted EPS $7.23, guide above $18.25 [3]; and July 16, 2026, Q2 revenue $112.0 billion, adjusted EPS $6.38, MCR 86.7% versus 89.4%, guide to $19.50-$20.00 adjusted, MCR 88.1% ±25 bps, cash flow about $24 billion from above $18 billion, buyback at least $5.0 billion from about $2.5 billion [4].

(c) Discrete risk, unresolved. DOJ criminal and civil probes of MA risk-adjustment billing, confirmed July 2025 [5], later reported to reach Optum Rx and physician pay [6]. Senate Judiciary report, Jan. 12, 2026 [7]. Amended shareholder complaint, D. Minn., filed Aug. 7 and reported Aug. 13, 2026 [8] — likely cause of ret_1m -5.1%.

Policy tailwind: CMS finalized CY2027 MA rates April 6, 2026 at +2.48% net (about +4.98% with risk-score trend) versus +0.09% proposed, and left the v28 risk model uncalibrated [9][10].

2. Is the impairment real?

None of the three percentiles describes reality.

P/E, 73rd percentile — a trough-EPS artifact. eps_base_ttm $15.55 is down 32.6% year over year, against 2025 adjusted EPS of $16.35 and 2024 adjusted $27.66 [2]. A 25.8x multiple on collapsed GAAP earnings says nothing; the engine left eps_ttm_flag null and caveats empty, and both are wrong. Discard that leg.

P/S, 13th percentile — an artifact the other way. 2026 revenue is guided below 2025 and Q2 grew 0.4%; net margin was 2.7% in 2025 and 4.9% in Q2 [2][4], below what the historical P/S embedded. A structurally lower margin deserves a lower P/S — repricing, not cheapness.

P/FCF, 23rd percentile — this is the entire band. The blend is the median of three legs, and P/FCF is the median at all three rungs: fair_band equals fair_band_by_basis.pfcf exactly. It rests on TTM FCF of $23.6 billion at 1.67x net income — but UNH attributed Q2's $11.1 billion operating cash flow (1.9x) partly to "the timing of a substantial government payment" [4], and the 2026 guide, about $24 billion on net earnings above $16.75 billion, implies roughly 1.4x.

What survives: 17.9x forward on $22.44 FY27 consensus; low_implied_fwd_pe 17.6 confirms the safety rung is 17.6x forward. Fair, not cheap.

Cyclical or structural? Both. Turning: Medicare trend below the initial ~10% assumption, Medicare margins above 3% for 2026, MCR guide cut 70 bps [4][11]. Structural: the recovery is bought with volume — MA membership down 965,000 since year-end 2025, about 1.1 million expected for the year; Optum Health value-based patients about -700,000; commercial trend "modestly above 11%" [4][11]. And CMS finalized excluding unlinked chart-review diagnoses from CY2027 risk scores [10] — aimed at the practice the Senate report and DOJ probes concern [7].

What the price implies. At the 9% hurdle from forward EPS: 3.58%/yr for a decade at a median exit, 4.64% at 20x — against management's 13%-16% long-term range, 13.24% consensus and a 0.54% trailing five-year CAGR. A low bar. But I can name the structural reason — an open criminal investigation into the source of MA revenue per member — and the p20 rung does not price it: $394.92 is 1.7% below spot.

The replay. Five closed entries, 40% beat SPY, median excess -0.5% — but two are overlapping 2020 COVID entries into a ripping market, and the one entry into a real crisis (May 8, 2025, 5th percentile) returned +2.5%, -29.8% excess, -37.9% drawdown, five days before guidance was pulled. Week-weighted, weeks_in_zone_median_excess_12m is +12.7% — opposite sign. Different now: guidance exists and has been raised twice; estimates are +8.1% in 90 days on 24 net raises; trend runs below plan, not above. Same: the DOJ.

3. Verdict and entry

WAIT. The only cheapness that survives scrutiny is 17.9x forward, and the engine's own margin-of-safety rung sits 1.7% below spot — you are being asked to underwrite an open criminal investigation for a discount a 51.8% 12-month move has already arbitraged away.

Entry rung: fair_band.low $394.92 (17.6x forward), armed only on an event gate: the Q3 print, Oct. 27, 2026, confirming MCR inside the 88.1% ±25 bps guide and a 2027 membership plan that stops the decline.

Tier: quality_compounder, 9%, as assigned. fwd_at_hurdle_median_exit 3.58% is achievable against 13.24% consensus. I am not raising the hurdle to 12% to express DOJ doubt; the return-hurdle policy prohibits it and the doubt belongs in §4.

  2. Cost cycle — Q3 MCR above 89.0%, or the FY26 MCR guide raised above 88.35%, at the Oct. 27, 2026 print → the turn was prior-period development, not trend.
  3. Revisions — fwd_eps_rev_90d ≤ -5% on any Monday signals.json refresh → recovery not durable.
  4. Volume — 2027 MA enrollment guided to fall more than the ~1.1 million expected in 2026, at the December 2026 investor conference or the Q4 release (late Jan. 2027) → repricing has become shrinkage.
  5. Stars — 2027 star ratings (CMS, expected early Oct. 2026): under 70% of MA members in 4+ star plans, versus 78% for 2026 → 2028 revenue impaired.
