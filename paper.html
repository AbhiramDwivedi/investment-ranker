Desk-note vetoes

Desk-note vetoes do not apply to this book, so it trades its ranking as computed.

This book is also published as machine-readable JSON.

How the paper books work

Orders are written on one close and fill at the raw closing price of the next trading session. A ranking therefore never trades at the price it was computed from.

Every fill is charged 10 basis points of the dollars traded, and buys are scaled down so cash never goes negative.

A missing price is never treated as a sale. A position closes only on a documented corporate action with a public source, at the documented proceeds. Anything else stays open as unresolved, the book's value becomes a range, and its performance status becomes unverified.

A leg with no price at its fill close stays pending and is retried at each later close. After three closes without a price, it is recorded as unfilled.

Orders are published before they fill. The machine-readable files hold the same orders, positions and ledger rows as the state files the books run from.

Record. The index of every book is published as machine-readable JSON. These are model books that hold no real money and use no personal data.
