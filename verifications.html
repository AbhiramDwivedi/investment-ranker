How a check works

The first check reads the income statement, cash flow statement and balance sheet in the filed documents. A trailing twelve-month figure is the latest fiscal year plus the current year to date minus the prior year to date.

A value matches when it is within 0.5% of the filing or within the filing's rounding. Each company is checked on the same 24 values, and any further value is shown but not counted.

Filings report no fourth-quarter share average, so the twelve-month diluted share rows repeat the model's own construction from filed inputs. They are a reproduction and not an independent test.

The second reviewer is a different model from a different company. It receives the first check's rows and claims, fetches the filings itself, and is told to look for mistakes.

Record. The same checks are published as machine-readable JSON, with the components of every calculation and the filing each came from. These checks use no personal data.
